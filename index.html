Industry Night Information

Step into an Inspiring Showcase


Together, let's celebrate creativity, innovation, and collaboration.

We are honoured to invite you to the showcase featuring the work of our Interactive Media Design fourth-semester students.

Discover the creativity and innovation of our students as they present the projects they have been working on this term, proudly highlighting their skills, teamwork, and dedication to the craft.

Alongside the group project showcase, you will also have the opportunity to explore individual student portfolios, a window into their unique styles, passions, and professional aspirations.

What it is all about?

  • Level 4 students partnered in designing and delivering a digital platform that preserves the story of the 427 Wing building, ensuring its history and the sacrifices of Canadian aviation veterans remain accessible for future generations.

  • Through Spotlight presentations, our IDP3 students will reveal their design journeys, sharing the creativity and problem-solving behind the projects developed in partnership with the Royal Canadian Air Force Association (Local 427 Wing).

  • Experience the creativity and dedication of our students as they showcase their portfolios, reflecting strong abilities in interactive media, UX design, web development, and more.

Interactive Media Design

The Interactive Media Design (IDP3) program at Fanshawe is a two-year (60-week) diploma that equips students with the skills to thrive in digital media, web design and development, 3D animation, multimedia production, UX/UI, and interactive design.

Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools such as Adobe Creative Cloud, Maxon, Figma, as well as open-source technologies, while exploring courses in design, programming, media management, and digital marketing.

Graduates are prepared for careers in design studios, app and web development, multimedia production companies, digital agencies, and technology consulting, among many other creative industries.
